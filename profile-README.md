## What we do

TechAura builds AI products, intelligent automation, and custom software for startups, SaaS companies, agencies, and service businesses in the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, and Europe.

We work in three pillars:

**Software** — Custom products, MVPs, and platforms built to ship and scale.

**Intelligence** — AI products, LLM agents, integrations, and data-driven features added to new or existing systems.

**Automation** — Workflows that remove manual work: lead handling, qualification, CRM updates, follow-up, booking, and reporting.

## Capabilities

- AI product development and MVPs
- AI integration into existing products and workflows
- AI agents and workflow automation (including n8n)
- System repair, improvement, and scaling
- Custom software for operations, sales, and customer support

## Flagship offer: AI Lead Automation

Our automation entry point for service businesses: a lead is captured, replied to instantly, qualified by AI, written to the CRM, followed up, and booked, without the team chasing every inquiry by hand, including after hours.

## How we work

1. Research and diagnose the real problem first
2. Map the smallest system that solves it
3. Build, integrate, and test in the real workflow
4. Measure outcomes: revenue, time saved, efficiency, conversion

No generic builds. Every system is designed around a business outcome.
